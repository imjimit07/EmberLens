04 / SCIENTIFIC VALIDATION

Harmonization lab

Compare MODIS standard processing with one selected VIIRS platform using matched, approximately 10 km grid cells per full ISO week.

Loading overlap analysis…
Method, validation criteria, and limitations

The experiment uses MODIS_SP and exactly one selected VIIRS standard-processing product (Suomi-NPP, NOAA-20, or NOAA-21). Different VIIRS missions are not pooled into one calibration target. Active-fire points are grouped by full ISO week and an approximately 10 km region-centered grid; occupied cells are counted as the analysis unit. Fire events and burned area are separate measures.

For real-data validation, each day of a candidate week must have a successful FIRMS API retrieval for both selected sources in the same region. Retrieved days are evidence of a completed request only. Archive CSVs without query provenance cannot establish zero-detection weeks.

A log-linear model is trained on earlier paired weeks and tested on the chronologically latest 25% (minimum six). Its test-set mean absolute error must beat a training-only fitted multiplicative baseline by at least 2% before being promoted. This tests cross-sensor agreement. Independent ground truth is outside this experiment. If the candidate fails, its estimates are excluded from calibrated outputs.

What do these bands mean?

Shaded band: observed variation between historical years. This is a descriptive band with no predictive interpretation.

Sensor difference: descriptive disagreement between instruments with different detection sensitivities. Ground-truth error requires independent validation.

Missing weeks: excluded from the analysis, never silently treated as zero detections.

Flags: retrospective and heuristic. These flags are intended for retrospective research only.
